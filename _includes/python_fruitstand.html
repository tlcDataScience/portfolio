Fruit Stand Inventory Management System

Description:

I took charge of managing a fruit stand where the daily supply of apples follows an Arithmetic Progression. I wrote a Python program to calculate how many apples were in stock on day 18 and the total number of apples in stock after 18 days.

What the program does:

The program starts with 40 apples on the first day and increases the stock by 6 apples every day. A for loop is used to calculate the daily stock and the total number of apples over 18 days.
